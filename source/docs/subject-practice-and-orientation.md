# Subject practice, assignment uploads and orientation

Published implementation: 1 November 2026.

Students buy reviewed subject questions for KSh 10 and protected uploaded tutorials for KSh 20. Each product grants six days from admin verification, scoped to the assigned teacher, class and subject; active tuition is also required. Exempt subjects and active assessments cannot open practice/tutorials. No access is sold when published content is absent. Manual Paybill receipt verification is available; this change does not enable live M-Pesa prompts. Ledger details, verification and financial summaries remain admin-only. Confirmed subject receipts contribute to the finance totals separately from tuition and registration.

Teachers can generate draft packs from all their published lessons in batches of 20. Publishing explanatory lesson notes also prepares a draft automatically. Existing drafts and reviewed packs survive retries without replacement. The generator is an extractive notes-based tool, not an external AI API. Admin receives 24 original JEZTECH revision-note draft packs (118 questions). Review, edit and publish each suitable pack before it becomes purchasable. This is not full syllabus coverage: missing notes must be supplied, and the live video library needs actual teacher uploads. Public KICD resources remain public; no third-party public video is repackaged as a protected sale.

In Topic assignments, select class/subject/topic to load questions from the available notes. Review answer keys and the school rating bands, then publish. Learners may answer online or upload a numbered CSV answer sheet with header `question,answer` and rows such as `1,A`. Every question needs one A–D answer. The server marks objective answers; clients cannot set marks. Plain TXT written submissions (maximum 30 KB) wait for the author/admin to award marks and explanatory feedback. Handwritten images and PDFs are not automatically interpreted. Scores and rankings lock after marking. Reports show submitted/assigned counts, mean percentage, school rating distribution and item accuracy for objective submissions; pending written work is excluded until reviewed. CSV result export includes ratings.

Rating thresholds are editable school practice settings saved with the draft before publication. Default CBC/CBE written-task bands use four expectation descriptors; Form 3 uses school letter bands. They are not KNEC national grades, full competency judgements, or certification. Practical competencies require observed evidence and the approved task rubric. Guidance: https://www2.knec.ac.ke/services/cba/ and https://www.knec.ac.ke/wp-content/uploads/2026/01/KCSE_Regulations_2024.pdf.

After admission approval and verified registration payment, a student can explicitly start one 12-hour orientation in Registration fee, Activate access or student Overview. During that window, tuition and subject resource charges are waived. It covers published resources available to the student's class, subjects and assigned teacher, including protected clips without consuming welcome credit. The timer continues while away and cannot restart. Rejected registration revokes it immediately. It does not create a subscription payment, teacher earning or a financial collection. Normal access expiry and subject charges resume afterwards. Existing active-assessment safeguards still apply.

Validation covers payment prices and uniqueness, verification roles, class/subject and expiry checks, answer-key privacy, generation retries, CSV scoring, written review, school ratings, financial totals, orientation prerequisites, one-time expiry, revocation and scoped lesson content. Signed-in browser and real payment end-to-end testing still require an authorized test session and configured production payments.

Orientation duration updated to 12 hours on 1 November 2026. Existing shorter grants are extended to original started_at + 12 hours. Grants already beyond that end time remain expired; the start action still cannot restart a used grant.
