# Live learner board and tutorial presentation

Teachers see **Learners in this lesson** above the camera gallery. It lists all online learners in pages of 50, including learners who keep their cameras hidden. Select **View in gallery** to find that learner in a server-media lesson. Connect the teacher broadcast first; the learner must also join the media gallery. For small peer lessons, all connected participants remain on screen.

Server-media galleries support searching connected participants by name. The teacher remains pinned; up to 11 other cameras are downloaded on a page. This keeps 500-plus learner rooms from downloading every video simultaneously. Attendance remains based on recent lesson heartbeats, not on camera use or proof of attention.

Teachers and learners use **Show my video** and **Hide my video** during the lesson. Hiding stops camera publication; showing requires the user's browser permission. In small peer lessons, users can now enable a camera after joining without leaving. School permission confirmation still applies to school-room capture. Teachers cannot remotely force a learner's camera on.

## Share a tutorial

1. Upload a tutorial in the dashboard's Learning videos section.
2. In a live classroom, search your tutorial titles, choose the clip, enter its length in seconds and choose its display order.
3. Select **Share & present tutorial** to share it and open your player, initially paused at the beginning. Press Play to start. Play, pause and seek update the class presentation.
4. Learners select **Join teacher's tutorial** to open the current position, then enable playback if their browser requires a click. They may turn off **Follow teacher's presentation when active** for independent review.

**Share for learners to open** shares without starting a presentation. Re-sharing the same tutorial updates the existing lesson entry. **Withdraw clip** removes it and ends its class presentation. Lesson expiry, registration, subject exemptions, tutorial entitlements and video-credit checks continue to apply. Sharing never purchases content or reserves a learner's video credit automatically. Active assessments continue to block learning clips.

Live camera/audio quality and browser permission behaviour must be checked with the actual teacher and learner devices. This release does not record class camera feeds.
